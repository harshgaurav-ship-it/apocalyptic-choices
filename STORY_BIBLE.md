# Mumbai: Afterlight — Story Bible v1

## Core promise

An original 2D cinematic survival drama in Mumbai. The player is Harsh, whose choices protect people, cost resources, alter trust, and create consequences that return later. The first playthrough targets about three hours across five compact episodes.

## Main cast

| Character | Role | Inner conflict | What they remember |
|---|---|---|---|
| Harsh | Player character | Protecting Kavya versus helping strangers | Every moral choice |
| Kavya | Harsh's younger sister | Wants safety without becoming cruel | Broken promises and acts of care |
| Kabir | Harsh's old friend | Truth versus the need to keep moving | Evasion, honesty, and risk |
| Zoya | Dispensary medic | Saving the many versus saving the known | How Harsh uses scarce resources |
| Raghav | Former station-control worker | Guilt over what the system knew | Whether Harsh opens the door to him |

## Episodes

### 1. The Last Train

Harsh reaches Kavya at Dadar as the last local stalls. Act I covers the platform, station escape, dispensary, repeater, and Service Gate. Act II reveals what Raghav knows: station control received an evacuation order that was never broadcast.

### 2. No Way Home

The group heads north through closed wards and flooded roads. The apparent safest guide sells routes to a hostile survivor crew. The player decides whether to expose the betrayal early or preserve group stability.

### 3. The Other Side

The group reaches a guarded settlement. It is safe because it quietly sends sick people back outside. Zoya's medical judgment and Kavya's moral line collide.

### 4. Mumbai

The settlement fails during the monsoon. The survivors cross the city toward a rumored evacuation ferry. Relationships decide who stays, who leaves, and who is still willing to follow Harsh.

### 5. Afterlight

The ferry has fewer places than survivors. The ending grows from accumulated trust, resource use, truth, and the people Harsh chose to protect.

## Persistent state

- Relationship trust: Kavya, Kabir, Zoya, Raghav
- Resources: group supplies and carried items
- Memory flags: Rohan, Leela, first route, radio response, Service Gate choice
- Consequence rule: every major decision must affect dialogue, access, relationships, resources, or the ending; choices never exist only for flavor.

## Production guardrails

- Keep all story, names, dialogue, and art original.
- Preserve the ₹0 rule: local HTML/CSS/JavaScript, freely available tooling only, and no subscriptions.
- Add reusable systems before branching content where possible.
